###### 1. Outdated Look &amp; Feel


If your site looks like it hasn’t been updated in years, visitors may assume your business is outdated too. Modern, clean visuals build trust.


###### 2. Poor Mobile Experience


Over 60% of web traffic comes from mobile devices. If your site isn’t responsive or easy to navigate on phones, you’re losing potential customers.


###### 3. Slow Load Times


People won’t wait around for a slow site. If pages take more than 3 seconds to load, you risk high bounce rates and lower search rankings.


###### 4. Low Conversion Rates


If visitors aren’t filling out forms, buying products, or engaging with your content, your design and user journey may need a rethink.


###### 5. Difficult to Update


If making small changes requires coding knowledge or outside help, it’s time for a modern CMS and a more flexible design.
