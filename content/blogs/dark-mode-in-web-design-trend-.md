###### What is Dark Mode?


Dark mode uses a dark background with light text and UI elements, creating a sleek, high-contrast design that’s easier on the eyes in low-light environments.


###### Why It’s Trending


###### 1. Modern Aesthetic


Dark mode gives websites and apps a sleek, premium look that appeals to modern audiences.


###### 2. Eye Comfort


It reduces glare in low-light settings, making content easier to read at night.


###### 3. Battery Efficiency


On OLED and AMOLED screens, dark mode can **save battery life** by using less power to display black pixels.


###### 4. Brand Differentiation


Offering a dark mode option can make your product feel **more innovative and user-focused**.


###### 5. Popular Demand


Big brands like **Apple, Google, Twitter, and YouTube** have embraced dark mode — and users now expect it as an option.


###### Best Practices for Implementing Dark Mode


- Ensure Readability → Use high-contrast text and avoid pure black (#000) to reduce strain.
- Adapt Brand Colors → Adjust your palette for dark backgrounds to maintain visual harmony.
- Give Users Control → Let them toggle between light and dark mode.
- Test for Accessibility → Check color contrast ratios to meet WCAG standards.
